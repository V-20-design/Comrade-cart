Privacy Policy

1. Data We Collect

To fulfill your deliveries in Kutus, we collect basic transactional information, including:

  • Your name (for rider delivery verification).
  • Phone number (for M-Pesa payments and rider contact).
  • Location details (hostel/apartment name and room number).
  • Cart contents and purchase history.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your details are used solely to prepare orders, calculate delivery routes in Kutus, contact you regarding delivery status, and coordinate M-Pesa STK transactions. We do not sell or share student details with external tracking companies.

3. Age Verification

Orders containing liquor products require age confirmation. Delivery riders will check university student IDs or national IDs at the delivery point to ensure you are 18 years of age or older.

4. Contact Us

If you wish to update or delete your customer order logs, contact our privacy desk at privacy@comradecart.co.ke.

Terms & Conditions

1. Service Availability

ComradeCart operates deliveries in Kutus Town and surrounding student zones. Our active service hours are strictly from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM daily. Orders placed outside this window will be scheduled for delivery the following morning starting at 8:00 AM.

2. Pricing & Delivery Fees

Product prices are aggregated from local Kutus vendor shops. We charge a flat delivery rate of KES 50 for students. Prices are subject to slight market adjustments, but the final cart price at checkout is what you pay.

3. Payment Methods

All payments must be completed prior to or upon delivery arrival via Safaricom M-Pesa. Standard M-Pesa transactions are simulated via our checkout platform for confirmation. Cash on delivery is accepted only for non-alcohol, small grocery orders.

4. Alcohol Delivery Restriction

By ordering liquor or alcohol products, you agree that you are over 18 years old. ComradeCart supports responsible drinking. Riders are legally required to cancel the alcohol portion of any order if the buyer cannot verify their legal age on delivery.

5. Cancellations & Refunds

Students can cancel orders before the preparing stage has finished. Once the status shows "Rider Dispatched," cancellation is no longer possible. Refunds for damaged or missing items will be credited back via M-Pesa within 12 hours.
